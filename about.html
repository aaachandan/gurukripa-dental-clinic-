Our Story

Our Journey to Healthy Smiles

Gurukripa Dental Clinic was founded with a singular vision — to make world-class dental care accessible, affordable, and comfortable for every patient in Raipur. Established by Dr. Shruti Sinha, the clinic began as a modest practice driven by passion and has grown into one of the most trusted dental healthcare destinations in the region.

Over the years, we have treated thousands of patients, transforming their smiles and improving their quality of life. Our clinic has evolved with cutting-edge technology, a larger team of skilled professionals, and a patient-first approach that puts comfort, transparency, and excellence at the heart of everything we do.

Today, Gurukripa Dental Clinic stands as a symbol of trust, quality, and innovation in dental care — offering everything from routine checkups to advanced surgical procedures, all under one roof.

Dr. Shruti Sinha

BDS, MDS

Consultant Dental Surgeon & Implantologist

Dr. Shruti Sinha is the founder and lead dental surgeon at Gurukripa Dental Clinic. With over 8 years of clinical experience, she has earned a reputation for delivering painless, precise, and compassionate dental care. Her expertise spans general dentistry, cosmetic dentistry, root canal treatments, and dental implants.

She believes in continuous education and stays updated with the latest advancements in dental technology and techniques. Her patient-first philosophy ensures that every individual who walks into the clinic receives personalised attention, clear treatment explanations, and world-class care in a comfortable environment.
