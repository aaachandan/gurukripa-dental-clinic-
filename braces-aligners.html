FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions? We have answers about braces and aligners.

How much do braces cost in Raipur?

The cost of braces at Gurukripa Dental Clinic depends on the type you choose. Metal braces start from ₹15,000 per arch, ceramic braces from ₹25,000 per arch, and clear aligners from ₹35,000 per arch. We offer flexible payment plans to make treatment affordable for everyone. A full consultation will help determine the best option and exact cost for your case.

How long does orthodontic treatment take?

Treatment duration varies depending on the complexity of your case and the type of braces chosen. On average, treatment lasts between 12 to 24 months. Metal and ceramic braces typically take 18-24 months, while clear aligners may take 6-18 months for mild to moderate cases. Your dentist will provide a personalized timeline during your consultation.

Do braces hurt?

Modern orthodontic treatments are much more comfortable than in the past. You may feel mild pressure or soreness for 2-3 days after each adjustment session, which is a sign that your teeth are moving. This discomfort is easily managed with over-the-counter pain relievers and usually subsides quickly. Clear aligners tend to cause even less discomfort than traditional braces.

Is there an age limit for braces?

There is no age limit for orthodontic treatment. Both children (age 8+) and adults can benefit from braces and aligners. In fact, more adults than ever are seeking orthodontic treatment to improve their smiles. We offer age-appropriate options for everyone, from teens to seniors, with clear aligners being particularly popular among adult patients.

How do I care for my teeth with braces?

Good oral hygiene is essential during orthodontic treatment. Brush after every meal using a soft-bristled toothbrush and fluoride toothpaste. Floss daily using a floss threader or water flosser. Avoid sticky, hard, and sugary foods that can damage braces or cause cavities. Regular dental check-ups and professional cleanings every 6 months are also important to maintain healthy teeth and gums throughout treatment.

What is the difference between braces and clear aligners?

Braces use brackets and wires fixed to your teeth to apply continuous pressure and gradually move them into position. Clear aligners are a series of removable, transparent trays that you replace every 1-2 weeks. Braces are more effective for complex cases, while aligners offer greater convenience, aesthetics, and easier oral hygiene. Your orthodontist will recommend the best option based on your specific needs and lifestyle.
