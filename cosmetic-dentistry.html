Cosmetic Dentistry FAQ

Common questions about cosmetic dental treatments

What cosmetic dentistry procedures do you offer?

We offer a full range of cosmetic treatments including porcelain veneers, composite bonding, professional teeth whitening, gum contouring, dental crowns, inlays/onlays, and smile makeovers. During your consultation, we recommend the best options for your specific goals.

How long does teeth whitening last?

Professional teeth whitening results typically last 6-12 months depending on your oral habits. Avoiding staining foods and beverages like coffee, tea, and tobacco will prolong results. Touch-up treatments are available to maintain your bright smile.

Are porcelain veneers permanent?

Porcelain veneers are a long-term cosmetic solution lasting 10-15 years with proper care. The procedure involves minimal enamel removal, making it irreversible. However, veneers are highly durable and maintain their appearance for many years.

Does cosmetic dentistry hurt?

Most cosmetic procedures are performed under local anesthesia, ensuring your comfort throughout. Some patients may experience mild sensitivity after treatment, particularly with whitening, but this typically resolves within 24-48 hours.

How do I choose between veneers and bonding?

Veneers offer superior durability, stain resistance, and aesthetics for major transformations, while bonding is more affordable and can be completed in a single visit for minor repairs. Our cosmetic dentist will help you decide based on your needs, budget, and goals.
