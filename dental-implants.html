Dental Implants — The Gold Standard in Tooth Replacement

Dental implants are the closest replacement to natural teeth. A small titanium post is surgically placed into your jawbone, acting as an artificial tooth root. Once healed, a custom-made porcelain crown is attached — giving you a tooth that looks, feels, and functions just like the real thing.

Unlike bridges or dentures, implants are a permanent solution that preserves your jawbone, maintains facial structure, and never compromises adjacent healthy teeth. With a success rate of over 97%, dental implants are trusted by millions worldwide.

At Gurukripa Dental Clinic, Dr. Shruti Sinha uses advanced guided implant surgery and premium materials to deliver exceptional results with minimal discomfort.

Step-by-Step

Your Implant Journey

From initial consultation to your brand new smile — here is what to expect at every stage

1

Comprehensive Consultation

We evaluate your oral health with digital X-rays and CBCT scans to assess bone density and plan the perfect implant placement strategy.

2

Implant Placement

The titanium implant post is gently placed into your jawbone under local anaesthesia — a precise, minimally invasive procedure.

3

Healing & Osseointegration

Over the next 3-6 months, the implant fuses naturally with your jawbone (osseointegration), creating a rock-solid foundation.

4

Crown Attachment

Once healed, a custom-crafted porcelain crown is securely attached to the implant — matching your natural teeth perfectly.

5

Follow-Up & Aftercare

We schedule regular follow-ups to monitor healing and provide guidance on maintaining your new implant for a lifetime.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about dental implants

How much do dental implants cost?

The cost of a dental implant depends on several factors — number of implants, need for bone grafting, type of crown, and the complexity of your case. At Gurukripa Dental Clinic, we offer transparent pricing starting from competitive rates. During your consultation, we provide a detailed, no-obligation estimate tailored to your needs. We also offer easy payment plans to make treatment affordable.

Is the implant procedure painful?

Not at all. The procedure is performed under local anaesthesia, so you will not feel any pain during placement. Most patients report that it is less uncomfortable than a tooth extraction. After the anaesthesia wears off, any mild discomfort can be easily managed with over-the-counter pain relievers. Our team ensures you are completely comfortable throughout the process.

How long does the entire implant process take?

The full process typically takes 3 to 6 months from start to finish. This includes the initial consultation, implant placement surgery, a healing period of 3-4 months for osseointegration (bone fusion), and finally the crown attachment. In some cases where immediate loading is possible, we may place a temporary crown on the same day as the implant.

What is the success rate of dental implants?

Dental implants have a success rate of over 97% in healthy individuals, making them one of the most predictable and reliable surgical procedures in dentistry. Success depends on factors like good oral hygiene, adequate bone density, overall health, and regular follow-ups. With proper care, implants can last a lifetime.

How do I care for my dental implant?

Caring for your implant is just like caring for natural teeth — brush twice daily, floss regularly, and visit us for routine check-ups. We recommend using a soft-bristle toothbrush and non-abrasive toothpaste. Special implant-specific floss or interdental brushes help clean around the implant. Avoid chewing on hard objects and maintain regular professional cleanings every 6 months.

Am I a candidate for dental implants?

Most adults with good general and oral health are suitable candidates for dental implants. You need sufficient jawbone density to support the implant and healthy gums free of periodontal disease. Smokers and individuals with uncontrolled diabetes or certain medical conditions may require additional evaluation. A comprehensive consultation with Dr. Shruti Sinha will determine the best treatment plan for you.
