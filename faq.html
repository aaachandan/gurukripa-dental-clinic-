Dental Implants FAQ 6 Questions

Most patients report minimal discomfort during the implant procedure as it is performed under local anaesthesia. Post-operative pain is typically mild and can be managed with over-the-counter pain relievers.

With proper care and oral hygiene, dental implants can last 15 to 25 years or even a lifetime. They are made of titanium which is biocompatible and fuses with the jawbone.

Anyone with healthy gums and sufficient jawbone density can be a candidate. Patients with conditions like diabetes or who smoke may need additional evaluation.

The full process takes 3 to 6 months. It includes consultation, implant placement (surgery), osseointegration (healing period of 3-6 months), and finally placing the crown.

Brush twice daily, floss regularly, and visit your dentist every 6 months. Implants require the same care as natural teeth to prevent peri-implantitis.

The cost ranges from Rs. 20,000 to Rs. 60,000 per implant depending on the brand, type of crown, and any additional procedures like bone grafting required.

Root Canal FAQ 5 Questions

Root canal treatment is done under local anaesthesia so the procedure itself is not painful. It actually relieves the pain caused by an infected tooth.

Most root canal treatments can be completed in 1 to 2 sittings. Complex cases with severe infection may require 3 sittings.

Root canal treatment costs between Rs. 3,000 to Rs. 12,000 depending on the tooth and the type of crown chosen.

A successfully root-canaled tooth with proper crown placement can last a lifetime with good oral hygiene and regular dental visits.

You can resume normal activities immediately. Mild soreness may last 2-3 days and can be managed with pain relievers.

Braces & Aligners FAQ 5 Questions

Braces can be worn at any age. However, the ideal age for orthodontic treatment is between 10 to 14 years when the mouth and jaws are still developing.

Braces treatment typically takes 18 to 36 months depending on the complexity of the case and the type of braces used.

Metal braces are more affordable and effective for complex cases, while clear aligners are removable and virtually invisible but better suited for mild to moderate misalignment.

Some discomfort is normal after adjustment appointments. This typically lasts 2-3 days. Over-the-counter pain relief can help.

Yes, wearing a retainer after braces is crucial to prevent teeth from shifting back to their original position.

Teeth Whitening FAQ 5 Questions

Professional teeth whitening results can last 6 months to 2 years, depending on your diet, oral hygiene, and lifestyle habits.

Yes, professional teeth whitening under dental supervision is completely safe. We use clinically tested bleaching agents that do not harm enamel.

Yes, yellow teeth respond best to whitening treatments. However, grey or brown discolouration may not respond as well and alternative treatments like veneers may be recommended.

Professional teeth whitening costs between Rs. 5,000 to Rs. 15,000 depending on the technique used and the number of sessions required.

We provide custom home whitening kits with professional-grade gel. Over-the-counter products are less effective and may cause sensitivity if used incorrectly.

General Dentistry FAQ 5 Questions

You should visit the dentist every 6 months for a routine check-up and professional cleaning to maintain optimal oral health.

Brush twice a day with fluoride toothpaste, floss daily, limit sugary foods and drinks, and schedule regular dental visits every 6 months.

Impacted or partially erupted wisdom teeth that cause pain, infection, or crowding should be removed. Our dentist will evaluate with an X-ray.

Bleeding gums are usually caused by plaque buildup leading to gingivitis. Other causes include aggressive brushing, vitamin deficiencies, or certain medications.

Modern digital X-rays use very low radiation. They are safe for adults and children. Pregnant women should inform the dentist before any X-ray.
