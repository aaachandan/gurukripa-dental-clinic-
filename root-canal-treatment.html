Overview

What is Root Canal Treatment?

Root Canal Treatment (RCT) is a dental procedure designed to save an infected or severely damaged tooth by removing the infected pulp, cleaning the inside of the tooth, and sealing it to prevent further infection. At Gurukripa Dental Clinic, we specialize in painless root canal treatments using advanced rotary endodontics and modern anesthesia techniques.

Dr. Shruti Sinha and our expert team ensure that your RCT experience is comfortable, efficient, and virtually pain-free. By preserving your natural tooth, you avoid the need for extraction and maintain your natural bite and smile.

Procedure

Root Canal Treatment Steps

Our 5-step root canal procedure ensures a comfortable, effective, and lasting result.

1

Examination

We perform a thorough examination with digital X-rays and RVG to assess the infection and plan the treatment precisely.

2

Anesthesia

Local anesthesia is administered to ensure a completely pain-free experience throughout the procedure.

3

Cleaning

The infected pulp is removed and the root canals are thoroughly cleaned and disinfected using rotary instruments.

4

Filling

The cleaned canals are filled with a biocompatible material (gutta-percha) and sealed to prevent re-infection.

5

Crown

A custom dental crown is placed over the treated tooth to protect it and restore full chewing function.

Benefits

Why Choose Root Canal Treatment

Root canal treatment offers numerous advantages over tooth extraction and other alternatives.

Pain Relief

Immediately eliminates the severe toothache caused by infected pulp, providing instant relief from dental pain.

Saves Natural Tooth

Preserves your natural tooth structure, avoiding extraction and maintaining your natural bite alignment.

Prevents Infection

Removes bacteria from the infected root canal, preventing the spread of infection to surrounding teeth and gums.

Restores Function

After treatment and crown placement, you can chew and bite normally without discomfort or restriction.

Long-lasting

With proper care and oral hygiene, a root canal-treated tooth can last a lifetime, making it a permanent solution.

Cost-Effective

Root canal treatment is more affordable than tooth extraction followed by implants or bridges in the long run.

A typical root canal treatment is completed in 1 to 2 appointments. Each appointment usually lasts between 45 to 60 minutes. Complex cases involving multiple canals or severe infection may require additional visits. We offer single-visit RCT for suitable cases.

Mild sensitivity or discomfort for a few days is normal and can be managed with over-the-counter pain relievers. Avoid chewing on the treated tooth until the permanent crown is placed. Maintain good oral hygiene and attend follow-up visits as recommended by your dentist.

Wait until the numbness from local anesthesia wears off completely (usually 1-2 hours) before eating to avoid accidentally biting your cheek or tongue.
