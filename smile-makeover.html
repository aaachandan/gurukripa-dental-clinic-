Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about smile makeover treatment

What is a smile makeover?

A smile makeover is a customized combination of cosmetic dental procedures designed to improve the appearance of your smile. It may include teeth whitening, veneers, crowns, dental bonding, gum contouring, or orthodontic treatment depending on your specific needs and goals.

How long does a smile makeover take?

The duration varies based on the procedures involved. A simple makeover with whitening and bonding may take 1-2 visits, while complex cases involving veneers or orthodontics may take several weeks to months. We provide a timeline during your initial consultation.

Is a smile makeover painful?

Most procedures are performed under local anesthesia, ensuring minimal discomfort. Some patients may experience mild sensitivity after treatment, which typically subsides within a few days. Our team ensures your comfort throughout the process.

How long do smile makeover results last?

With proper oral hygiene and regular dental check-ups, smile makeover results can last 5-10 years or more. Porcelain veneers and crowns are highly durable, while whitening results may require occasional touch-ups. We provide detailed aftercare instructions.

Am I a candidate for a smile makeover?

Most adults with good oral health are candidates. Ideal candidates have healthy gums and sufficient tooth structure. We evaluate your oral health during the consultation and recommend the best approach. Even patients with existing dental work can benefit from a smile makeover.
