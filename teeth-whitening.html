Professional Teeth Whitening

At Gurukripa Dental Clinic, our professional teeth whitening treatment helps you achieve a brighter, more confident smile in just one visit. Using advanced bleaching agents and precise application techniques, we safely remove deep stains caused by coffee, tea, tobacco, and aging.

Our certified dental professionals tailor every treatment to your unique dental profile, ensuring optimal results with minimal sensitivity. Whether you're preparing for a special occasion or simply want to boost your everyday confidence, our teeth whitening solution delivers dramatic, natural-looking results.

Transform your smile in as little as 60 minutes with our clinically proven whitening system.

How It Works

Our streamlined teeth whitening process in just four simple steps

1

Consultation

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about teeth whitening

Yes. Professional teeth whitening at Gurukripa Dental Clinic is completely safe. The bleaching agents we use are specifically formulated to minimize enamel impact while effectively removing stains. Our dentists apply protective barriers to your gums and soft tissues to ensure maximum safety throughout the procedure.

Results typically last 12 to 18 months with proper aftercare. Avoiding staining foods and beverages (coffee, tea, red wine) for the first 48 hours, maintaining good oral hygiene, and scheduling regular dental cleanings will help extend the longevity of your whitening results. We also offer custom take-home kits for occasional touch-ups.

Some patients experience mild temporary sensitivity during or after treatment, which typically subsides within 24–48 hours. We use desensitizing agents and can adjust the concentration to ensure your comfort. Most patients report little to no discomfort during the procedure.

Most patients achieve 5–8 shades of improvement after a single session, depending on the current condition of their teeth and the nature of the staining. Our doctor will provide a realistic expectation during your initial consultation using our shade guide.

Teeth whitening is suitable for most adults with healthy teeth and gums. However, it is not recommended for pregnant or nursing women, children under 16, or individuals with severe gum disease, untreated cavities, or extreme tooth sensitivity. A consultation with our dentist will determine if you are an ideal candidate.
